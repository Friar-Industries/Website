Friar Industries
FTC Team 23707
Live, Laugh, Love Robotics

Friar Industries is a team of passionate robotics enthusiasts dedicated to designing, building, and programming robots. Our program is open to all students who share a keen interest in robotics and have a drive to explore and learn. We meet several times week to innovate ideas, collaborate on exiting projects, and participate in competitions. Join Friar Industries today and start building your future in engineering and technology!

Virtual Innovation Center

inspiring Robotics
promoting FIRST
Educating ROBLOX

At Friar Industries, we're passionate about spreading the values of FIRST Robotics and inspiring the next generation of engineers, innovators, and problem solvers. Through our unique Roblox game, we've created a virtual replica of our school's lab where our club meetings are held. Players can simulate and engage with the real-world challenges of the FIRST Tech Challenge. By blending creativity, teamwork, and technical skills, we aim to expand the excitement of robotics to a wider audience, cultivating a love for STEM and the spirit of FIRST in an interactive and fun way. Join us in exploring, learning, and growing with robotics—one game at a time!
